## The lead stories

1. **Three leaders, one table: the first trilateral** — Trump, Xi
   Jinping and Putin met for 90 minutes at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, on
   Thursday 19 November, on the sidelines of APEC — the first-ever
   US–China–Russia trilateral. Bilaterals beforehand: Trump–Putin
   ~40–42 minutes (Ukraine dominated, per the Kremlin), Trump–Xi
   about an hour. The joint "Declaration toward Peace" covered UN
   reform, increasing refined petroleum supply, ICC restraint, and
   the line "a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought",
   with a pledge of dialogue on reducing nuclear risks. No immediate
   breakthrough on Ukraine or Iran. *China — USA TODAY, 19 November;
   CNN, 19 November; Le Monde, 19 November; The Times live blog,
   19 November; White House statement via Foreign Exchanges.*
2. **World Court: Myanmar committed genocide** — The International
   Court of Justice ruled on Thursday, by nine votes to six, that
   Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in Rakhine State
   in 2017 — the first time the ICJ has found a state guilty of
   genocide. The 2017 campaign killed about 10,000 Rohingya and
   drove more than 730,000 into Bangladesh. The judgment orders
   restitution measures and investigations, with a further procedure
   on compensation; the case was brought by Gambia in 2019.
   *The Hague — Al Jazeera via Foreign Exchanges, 19 November;
   AP via Foreign Exchanges; Horizon, 19 November (4 sources);
   NYT/Guardian headlines, 19 November.*
3. **Anthropic soars, Wall Street sinks 1,100 points** — Anthropic
   (ANT) began trading on Nasdaq on Thursday: opened at $111.50,
   34% above the $83 IPO price set on Wednesday, and finished near
   $97. The broader market fell hard: the Dow lost 1,100.40 points
   (−2.19%) to 49,097 — below 50,000 for the first time since early
   June — the S&P 500 fell 2.02% to 7,175 for a sixth straight
   decline, and the Nasdaq fell 2.25%. All 11 S&P sectors fell;
   inflation worries (Philly Fed prices, Fed speakers) drove the
   sell-off. *US/Markets — Investopedia, 19 November; eOption
   market review, 19 November; investingLive wrap, 19 November.*
