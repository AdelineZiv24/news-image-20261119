1 China · Diplomacy — the lead

Three leaders, one table: the first trilateral

Trump, Xi and Putin met for 90 minutes in Shenzhen — the first US–China–Russia summit ever — and signed a “Declaration toward Peace”: UN reform, more refined oil, and that “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.” Nothing on Ukraine or Iran.

2 The Hague · Justice

World Court: Myanmar committed genocide

The ICJ ruled 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017 — the first time the court has found a state guilty of genocide. About 10,000 were killed; 730,000 fled to Bangladesh.

3 US · Markets

Anthropic soars, Wall Street sinks 1,100 points

Anthropic opened 34% above its $83 IPO price at $111.50 and closed near $97 — but the Dow fell 1,100 points, its sixth straight S&P 500 decline, as inflation fears gripped the day.

4 Asia · Bangladesh

Bangladesh burns: Yunus home set ablaze

Four were killed as violence hit 28 districts; Muhammad Yunus’s ancestral home was set on fire and paramilitaries deployed.

5 US · Culture

Van Gogh record: $190.4m for 1890 blossoms

“Châtaigniers en fleurs”, painted in Van Gogh’s last two months, sold at Sotheby’s on Wednesday — his most expensive work ever.

6 Global · Climate

COP31: ministers try to unblock Antalya

Ministers met in informal session on Wednesday evening to break the deadlock; a new draft text was under discussion on Thursday.

7 Global · Energy

IEA: Hormuz is energy’s gravest threat ever

The IEA’s annual report calls the Middle East crisis the greatest threat ever seen to energy security; Hormuz traffic runs at 42%.

8 Europe · Ukraine war

Kerch Bridge hit again; rail traffic halted

Ukraine struck the Crimea bridge for the third time this week; rail traffic was suspended. A Russian strike on Kyiv killed one.

9 Global · Games

GTA VI launches worldwide after two delays

Rockstar’s return to Vice City and Leonida went on sale at midnight local time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, 917 days after its reveal.

10 Asia · Mongolia

Mongolia’s president resigns; parliament accepts

Khürelsükh quit over rail-project claims he denies. The speaker serves as interim; an election must follow within four months.

11 Europe · Serbia

Student List takes Serbia’s parliament chair

Lecturer Aleksandar Čupić was elected speaker with 140 votes — and becomes interim president, with an election due this year.

12 Asia · Trade

South Korea’s exports pass $1 trillion, a first

Chip demand carried Korean exports through the $1 trillion mark for the first time, Seoul reported on Thursday.

13 Sport · Cricket

England beat Australia by 97 runs, take series

Duckett’s 133 set up Wednesday’s 97-run win in the Hobart decider, sealing the series 2–1.

14 Sport · Tennis

Alcaraz beats Medvedev, into Turin semi-finals

Carlos Alcaraz won 6–2 6–4 on Wednesday, an 11th straight victory; Tiafoe beat Cobolli 7–6 6–0 to put him in the semi-finals.

15 Global · Economy

IMF: public debt nears 100% of world GDP

Kristalina Georgieva warned global public debt will soon reach 100% of GDP — the highest since the Second World War.
